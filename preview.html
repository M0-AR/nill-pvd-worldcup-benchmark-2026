🕵️ Hidden patterns (exploratory — small N, verify before citing)

1. Long asymmetry ★ strongest

DAX longs +0.79R (+$19,766, n=30) vs shorts -0.26R (-$21,243, n=72). WTI longs +0.53R (+$14,297) vs shorts +0.01R. Power begets power — shorts subsidise longs even in a losing system.

2. Regime fragility

DAX-covid +0.95R vs post-2021 -0.25R. WTI pre-2020 +$16,838 vs covid -$2,021 vs post -$853. Gating (like pausing oil 4 months) is rational.

3. Rarity hierarchy

S1 81–100% of signals, S2 0–6, S3 0–1. Matches “few trades/week, S3 best but rarest”. S3 needs years of 15m + profile data.

4–5. Size puzzle + time shadow

OHLC calibration yields all-large bases → “small zones best” untestable without secret geometry. 15m bleed fits trading through 14:00 US-open shift. Both scope PhD follow-ups.

❓ FAQ

Can I get rich copying S1/S2/S3 as coded here?
No — and that is the contribution. Naive OHLC transcription fails costs and walk-forward. Copy the risk protocol and validation method, not the entries.
What actually transfers from the champion?
1% risk, R≥3, 30%-partial→breakeven, never move stops, stop after 3–4 losses, no evening holds, skip news, 2–3 month paper validation, personality fit.
What do I need for a real edge?
Weekly VAH/VAL zone geometry, footprint/tape confirmation, session/news gating, per-market fake calibration (DAX ~10pts vs ES 1–2pts), trailing under new fair value. See roadmap in README §10.
